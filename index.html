CPF LIFE in 60 seconds

Your foundation, explained simply.

At 55, your CPF savings form a Retirement Account. From 65, CPF LIFE turns it into a monthly payout for as long as you live, so you never outlive it.

From 65The earliest age CPF LIFE payouts start. You can defer up to 70 for higher payouts.
For lifePayouts continue for as long as you live, however long that is.
